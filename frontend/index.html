About this data

India Insights is a current-only view of a limited configured dataset, not the full Indian job market: postings older than the configured freshness window, or without a posting date, are hidden by default. Bengaluru Jobs is a separate Bengaluru-only feed of explicit Bengaluru tech roles; it has its own opt-in control for older or unknown-date listings. When the analytics API is unavailable, this dashboard uses clearly labelled illustrative sample data and never invents jobs.

Configured company career boards use Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby. Coverage is limited to configured companies and sources, not every opening. Missing posting dates remain unknown. A recent posting is not a guarantee that the role is still open; verify current availability and apply on the original company listing. Fields such as salary, experience, skills, and work mode may be marked unavailable when the employer does not provide them.
